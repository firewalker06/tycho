# Code Of Conduct

HQ contributors are expected to keep project spaces respectful, technical, and
focused on improving the software.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence and clear reproduction
  steps.
- Keep reviews focused on code, docs, behavior, tests, and user impact.
- Respect privacy. Do not post private paths, logs, tokens, screenshots, agent
  transcripts, or company-specific data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, slurs, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic disruption.
- Bad-faith security reports or attempts to access systems without consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation
when behavior harms the project or its contributors.
